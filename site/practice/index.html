The studio & venture

Areas of practice.

We scout talent and work with artists directly, or partner with record labels, galleries, and booking agencies. In either case, the artist enters the engagement willingly, on their own initiative or through a representative. Every engagement begins with an agreed scope and an upfront monthly Stewardship Subscription or fixed project fee.

Our commitment

Retainer terms.

Focused intervention. Space to execute.

The monthly Stewardship Subscription keeps studio capacity available between interventions. We come close to observe and edit, then step away while the artist executes on their own timeline. Wild-Lake takes no percentage of artist earnings or ownership of their intellectual property.

  1. 01

    Ownership

    The client retains ownership of their work. The Studio retains its proprietary frameworks and licenses delivered framework materials for the client’s practice. Public-facing writing transfers to the client upon final settlement.

  2. 02

    Availability & interventions

    The client triggers an intervention when it is needed. We agree response times, reserved capacity, and the scope of each intervention in advance. Periods of absence are part of the engagement, giving the artist room to work independently. Their consent and creative control remain explicit throughout.

  3. 03

    Fees

    The subscription is paid monthly in advance for reserved availability across the agreed cadence. Project commissions carry a fixed fee. We agree additional scope before work begins.
