Privacy Protocol

Contacting the studio

The optional contact form prepares a draft in your email app. It does not submit the form to a studio database. You review and send the message yourself.

Information you send by email is shared with the studio and handled through the email services involved. Include only what you want to share in an initial inquiry.

For questions about information you have shared, email info@wildlakestudio.com.

External services

The site serves its fonts, styles, scripts, and interactive tools locally. External platforms are reached only when you follow their links and have their own privacy practices. This page describes the studio’s contact form; it does not replace the privacy information for those services or for individual tools.
