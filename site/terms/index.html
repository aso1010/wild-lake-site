Terms of Practice

Wild Lake Studio LLC (“Studio”) operates as an independent talent architecture, sovereign A&R, and systems strategy practice. Engagement with the Studio is governed by the structural parameters and intellectual property boundaries detailed below.

1. Engagement & Retainer Mechanics

  • Retainer Structure: Studio engagements operate on an upfront monthly Stewardship Subscription (retainer) or fixed project fee basis. Work commences only upon receipt of cleared retainer funds.
  • Carrying Capacity & Scope: The Stewardship Subscription reserves studio availability and structural stewardship between client-triggered interventions. Response times, intervention scope, and reserved capacity are defined in the engagement agreement. Continuous daily deliverables and recurring meetings are included only where expressly agreed. Unused studio hours or advisory allocations do not roll over into subsequent billing cycles.
  • Termination & Notice: Either party may terminate an ongoing retainer engagement with thirty (30) days written notice. Retainers paid prior to notice are non-refundable; all active deliverables in progress through the final date of notice will be completed and handed over.

2. Intellectual Property & Sovereign Rights

  • Client IP Sovereignty: The Client retains complete, uncompromised ownership of all original work, creative assets, master recordings, manuscripts, software code, and underlying intellectual property (“Client IP”) introduced to or developed during the engagement. The Studio claims no ownership equity, royalty rights, or perpetual claims over Client IP unless explicitly structured under a separate, written joint-venture agreement.
  • Studio Frameworks & Methodology: The Studio retains exclusive ownership of its proprietary methodologies, intake diagnostics, systems architectures, operational frameworks, and strategic models (“Studio IP”). Engagement grants the Client a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to utilize delivered framework materials solely for the operation of their personal practice.
  • Critical Context & Public Framing: Writing, manifestos, artist statements, and strategic documentation authored by the Studio for public deployment become the exclusive property of the Client upon final retainer settlement.

3. Confidentiality & Governance

  • Information Security: The Studio maintains strict confidentiality regarding unreleased works, strategic roadmaps, financial arrangements, and personal client data. No client material will be disclosed, showcased, or distributed without prior written consent.
  • Portfolio & Archival Rights: Unless restricted by a signed Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA), the Studio reserves the right to archive finished, publicly released projects and list the engagement within private studio credentials decks.

4. Environmental Commitment

  • Freshwater & Land Allocation: A fixed portion of all studio retainer proceeds is allocated toward public land preservation and freshwater defense initiatives. This allocation is managed directly by the Studio and creates no financial or tax liability for the Client.

5. Jurisdiction

These Terms of Practice are governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York.

For inquiries regarding custom engagement terms, joint-venture structures, or formal NDAs, contact legal@wildlakestudio.com.
