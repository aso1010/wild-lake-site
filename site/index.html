Our practice

How we work

Nature is brutalist.

Nothing in it is decorative, and everything carries weight. We build infrastructure for artists under that same principle.

Our attention stays with the material and the questions it holds. We help artists sharpen their craft and refine the work on their own terms.

Critique is one way we love an artist’s work. Close examination gives the artist something specific to work with as the practice develops.

The artist can challenge our interpretation or use it to make a different choice. Their response remains theirs.

Institutions commission the work on a fixed retainer. We agree the scope with the institution and the artist, including the artist’s consent and creative control. Our attention stays with the artist and the material through making and revision.

Our commitment

Retainer terms.

The institution commissions. The artist shapes the work.

Record labels, galleries, booking agencies, and cultural institutions engage us on fixed retainers. Wild-Lake takes no percentage of artist earnings or ownership of their intellectual property.

  1. 01

    Ownership

    Wild-Lake takes no rights in the artist’s work. We agree ownership of the infrastructure we build before the engagement begins.

  2. 02

    Scope

    The retainer defines the institution’s commission and the work agreed with the artist. The artist’s consent and creative control are explicit in that scope.

  3. 03

    Fees

    We agree the fee and scope with the commissioning institution and revisit them as the work develops.
