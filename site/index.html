Our practice

How we work

Nature is brutalist.

We dismantle the architecture of extraction. The artist survives the loop.

Nothing in it is decorative, and everything carries weight. We build infrastructure for artists under that same principle.

Clients come to Wild-Lake when the way they work has become unsustainable. The engagement is a controlled demolition, undertaken with the artist. We take the practice down to its studs, dismantling habits and brittle systems that can no longer carry the work. Then we rebuild around what the artist needs to operate with clarity.

Our attention stays with the material and the questions it holds. We help artists sharpen their craft and refine the work on their own terms.

Critique is one way we love an artist’s work. Close examination gives the artist something specific to work with as the practice develops.

We come close only to observe and edit, bringing absolute clarity to the raw material. Then we depart. The response, the execution, and the timeline remain entirely sovereign.

We build for asynchronous stamina. Our engagement alternates between high-density structural interventions and extended periods of total absence, giving the artist the isolation required to execute without surveillance.

Artists can engage us directly, or an institution can commission the work. We agree the scope with those involved, including the artist’s consent and creative control. Our attention stays with the artist and the material through making and revision.

Our commitment

Retainer terms.

Focused intervention. Space to execute.

The monthly Stewardship Subscription keeps studio capacity available between interventions. We come close to observe and edit, then step away while the artist executes on their own timeline. Wild-Lake takes no percentage of artist earnings or ownership of their intellectual property.

  1. 01

    Ownership

    The client retains ownership of their work. The Studio retains its proprietary frameworks and licenses delivered framework materials for the client’s practice. Public-facing writing transfers to the client upon final settlement.

  2. 02

    Availability & interventions

    The client triggers an intervention when it is needed. We agree response times, reserved capacity, and the scope of each intervention in advance. Periods of absence are part of the engagement, giving the artist room to work independently. Their consent and creative control remain explicit throughout.

  3. 03

    Fees

    The subscription is paid monthly in advance for reserved availability across the agreed cadence. Project commissions carry a fixed fee. We agree additional scope before work begins.
