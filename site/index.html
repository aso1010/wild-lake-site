Wild Lake Studio

Talent architecture, A&R administration, and artist stewardship out of Brooklyn. We work across music, visual art, literature, and film. We take 0% ownership and 0% revenue share.

Read Essay 001 → · Begin →

The 27 Club was a structural failure.

We replace the extraction of 360 deals with sovereign artist development. Visibility needs a structure that can carry it: sustainable working rhythms, clear boundaries, and long-horizon direction. The artist keeps ownership and creative control.

Ways to work together

01.0 // Talent architecture

We audit capacity, establish boundaries, and build the operating structure that sustains a creative practice.

02.0 // A&R administration

We organize repertoire, catalog stewardship, and release operations so complete bodies of work reach the world on agreed terms.

03.0 // Artist mentorship

We give close critique and creative direction, helping artists develop their work and make decisions without surrendering control.
