Engagements & Pricing

Engagements are structured to protect the artist and sustain the practice.

Wild Lake Studio, known as Wild-Lake, works on a cross-subsidy model. Established artists fund capacity for rising artists. No artist owns another artist’s work. All artists retain ownership and creative control.

Two rhythms, one practice

Choose a rhythm around the demands of the practice: reserved availability with occasional intensive work, or more frequent support while building a foundation.

Established artists

Retainer + intensive

For pop stars and established artists across disciplines, including those navigating touring, album cycles, exhibitions, productions, and large teams.

Reserved availability

The retainer keeps Argelys available for text and voice-note access between sessions. Responses arrive within an agreed window, rather than instantly. A limited number of urgent calls per quarter is agreed in advance.

Access provides structural advice and grounding. It does not include management or creative approval.

Focused intervention

A deep intensive takes place every several months to years, in person or remotely, according to the artist’s needs.

  • Pre-session intake.
  • One to two days of deep work: a structural honesty audit, movement, writing, listening, and an infrastructure check.
  • A post-session structural memo with next steps.

PricingRetainer + intensive fees agreed individually.

Rising artists

Subsidized Stewardship Subscription

For rising and developing independent artists building their foundation. Established artist retainers fund capacity for higher-frequency, ongoing support at a subsidized rate.

Ongoing development

  • Regular sessions, with a cadence agreed together.
  • Development Protocols, including active listening, automatic writing, movement, and vision quests.
  • Operational infrastructure setup: request gating, direct payment routing, custom domain feeds, and async communication boundaries.

Scope and participation

Eligibility is based on the artist’s practice and need, not solely on inability to pay. Clear scope, expectations, and participation are agreed together. Subsidized does not mean charity: this is a real engagement.

PricingSubsidized subscription rate agreed individually.

A sliding scale based on need and available capacity, with terms agreed individually.

What access means

Reserved attention works within clear boundaries. An engagement does not create continuous, around-the-clock availability.

  • Agreed response windows, including the scope and limits of urgent access.
  • Async-first communication, with time for independent execution.
  • Request gating for professional inquiries.
  • The artist can pause, decline, or change direction at any time.
  • Touch and co-regulation practices are optional and require explicit, ongoing consent.

Included in every engagement

  • The artist retains ownership and creative control.
  • A Stewardship Subscription, retainer, or fixed project fee structure agreed in advance.
  • No percentage of masters, publishing, or touring.
  • Direct audience relationships stay with the artist.

How it works

  1. Tell us about your work.
  2. We determine fit, rhythm, and scope.
  3. Agree on retainer or subscription terms.
