Engagements & Pricing

Engagements protect the artist and sustain the practice.

Wild-Lake operates a closed-loop cross-subsidy model. Paid retainers from established artists and institutional partners directly fund higher-frequency Stewardship Subscriptions for rising independent artists. That funding creates zero claim on the work, equity, or revenue of any artist supported. The practice funds this capacity through its own paid engagements rather than external capital or predatory backend deals.

Within each engagement, upfront flat or recurring fees reserve asynchronous studio capacity and focused interventions. Interventions are followed by uninterrupted absence for independent execution. The artist retains 100% of their IP, masters, publishing, and direct audience ownership. At an agreed review, artist and studio examine the result and refine the next intervention.

Two rhythms, one practice

The scope determines the rhythm: reserved availability with occasional intensive work, or higher-frequency support while building a foundation. Both reserve capacity for agreed interventions and protect time for independent execution.

Established artists

Retainer and intensive

For established artists across disciplines, or their managers, labels, galleries, and agencies navigating touring, album cycles, exhibitions, or large-scale productions.

Reserved availability

The retainer reserves text and voice-note access to Argelys Samuel Oriach, Founder and Artistic Director, within agreed response windows. A capped number of urgent calls per quarter is agreed in writing. Wild-Lake provides structural advice, critique, and grounding. Day-to-day management and creative sign-off remain with the artist and their team.

Focused intervention

One-to-two-day intensives take place in person or remotely, spaced across months or years according to the work and the artist's capacity. Each intervention is followed by independent execution and an agreed review.

  • Pre-session intake.
  • A structural honesty audit, somatic and editorial work, and an infrastructure audit.
  • A post-session structural memo recording observations, decisions, next steps, and the evidence to review.

PricingRetainer and intensive fees are agreed individually.

Rising artists

Subsidized Stewardship Subscription

Higher-frequency ongoing support for developing independent artists building foundational infrastructure. Established artist retainers underwrite the reserved capacity.

Ongoing development

  • Regular sessions on an agreed cadence, with independent execution between interventions.
  • Optional Development Protocols selected with the artist.
  • Full Operational Infrastructure deployment within the agreed scope: request gating, static edge sites, direct payment routing, custom domain feeds, and async calendar boundaries.

Scope and participation

Eligibility rests on artistic rigor and structural need, with financial capacity factored in. Subsidized engagements are full working partnerships with mutual accountability. Artist and studio agree the scope, participation, boundaries, and review points before beginning, then assess the work and revise interventions together.

PricingA sliding scale based on need and available capacity, with the rate agreed individually.

Fixed project fee & institutional commissions

Standalone Creative Infrastructure builds include single-file edge sites, request-gating architecture, and direct payment and publishing pipelines. Fixed commissions also cover critical framing essays and focused diagnostic audits for labels, galleries, or management rosters. Scope, deliverables, review criteria, and the flat fee are agreed in full before work starts.

Operational boundaries & access

  • Agreed response windows and written quarterly urgent-call caps define reserved access.
  • Async-first communication protects studio execution, with focused interventions followed by uninterrupted absence.
  • Programmatic request gating routes incoming professional inquiries through an agreed structured process.
  • Consent remains ongoing. The artist can pause or exit any protocol and change direction within the agreed engagement terms.
  • Any physical or co-regulation work requires explicit prior agreement and ongoing consent. Proximity and touch are optional.

Sovereign terms included in every engagement

  • The artist retains 100% ownership of their IP, masters, publishing, touring revenue, and direct audience lists, with full creative control.
  • Wild-Lake takes zero backend points or commissions.
  • Transparent upfront fee agreements specify the reserved capacity, scope, access boundaries, and review points.
  • A dedicated fraction of every paid retainer is committed to freshwater ecosystem and public land conservation. Review the responsibility commitments.

How an engagement begins

  1. Submit work and context.
  2. Diagnostic scoping via Universe API principles: identify the actual bottleneck, the assets available, and the intervention to test.
  3. Written agreement on retainer, subscription, or fixed fee, including scope, response windows, and review criteria.

After the intervention, the artist executes independently. At the agreed review, artist and studio assess the result and refine the next scope.
