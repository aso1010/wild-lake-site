## Practice and engagement

### Sovereign A&R

https://wildlakestudio.com/method/#term-sovereign-ar

Multidisciplinary Artist & Repertoire: how we scout talent and develop complete bodies of work across music, visual art, literature, and film through critique, direction, and critical framing. The artist retains ownership and creative control.

The artist retains 100% ownership and creative control. Wild-Lake takes 0% IP ownership and 0% revenue share.

[Talent architecture: The method](https://wildlakestudio.com/method/#method-talent-architecture)

Your practice is the asset. We find where it is breaking.

Talent architecture is a capacity and boundary audit for creators whose workload has outgrown them. We dismantle the habits that drain you, build an operating framework that protects your autonomy, and step back. You keep your IP, your income, and your decisions.

The core offering is undivided attention. Close study of the artist, the work, and the conditions shaping both. We observe, listen, and stay with the material long enough to offer a precise response.

We scout talent and work with artists directly, or partner with record labels, galleries, and booking agencies. In either case, the artist enters the engagement willingly, on their own initiative or through a representative.

Artist development works with the creator behind the work. Creative development works with raw material across mediums and stages. Audience development, critical writing, and channels of encounter are creative infrastructure.

The Stewardship subscription reserves availability between client-triggered interventions. Focused observation and editing alternate with periods of independent execution. Scope, response times, and reserved capacity are agreed in advance. Fixed project commissions and focused advisory engagements are also available.

Artists retain ownership and creative control. The studio takes no percentage of artist earnings. The studio retains its proprietary frameworks and licenses delivered framework materials for the client’s practice; public-facing writing transfers to the client upon final settlement. Refer to the Terms of practice for the full agreement boundaries.

### About our founder

https://wildlakestudio.com/about-founder/#founder-overview

About our founder Investigation. Experimentation. Invitation. Argelys Samuel Oriach · Founder & artistic director Argelys Samuel Oriach is a Dominican-born performance artist, artistic director, and systems architect based in Crown Heights, Brooklyn. He is the Founder and artistic director of Wild Lake Studio. He operates two laboratories: Seven Eighty Three (Lab 783), an independent systems design practice, and Wild Lake Studio, its research laboratory, which guides developing and independent artists. The studio operates from New York, Tampa, and Santiago, and works worldwide. Seven Eighty Three addresses macro-systems architecture. Wild-Lake works with the artist and the practice through sovereign A&R and talent architecture. Universe API unifies the two scales: identify the conditions, scope an intervention, observe it in use, and revise it against the result. Wild Lake Studio formalizes Argelys's fifteen-year independent practice. It began in his freshman year at Wesleyan University, when he was initiated into the Eclectic Society and started producing live shows, organizing cultural space, and working alongside artists. Across twelve or more multidisciplinary artists, that practice has combined structural stewardship, physical carrying capacity, and live production architecture. Personal website · LinkedIn

### The person and the work

https://wildlakestudio.com/about-founder/#founder-work

The person and the work Argelys builds infrastructure for artists on the same principles he applies to technical architecture: nothing decorative, and everything carries weight. He was born in the Dominican Republic to a mother who is a housekeeper and writer and a father who is a facility maintainer and musician. His structural impulse surfaced at eleven, when he taught himself 3D design software to draft his parents' dream home. An architect later used that file for construction. After the 2008 financial crash, he applied to Phillips Academy Andover to design the conditions of his own life on purpose. A method actor trained in the Alexander Technique, he spent the next sixteen years treating his body, voice, and daily life as a single structural instrument. In 2010 he attended Northwestern University's National High School Institute (Cherubs) theatre program and played Nick in a stage adaptation of The Handmaid's Tale. From there he studied somatic weight, posture, and human attention by inhabiting roles across institutions, physical staging, and corporate infrastructure. That foundation fueled a twelve-year trajectory building organic growth systems. He served as Cynthia Nixon's social media manager for her 2018 campaign for governor of New York, then moved to architecting sovereign digital frameworks from first principles. He went on to build brand infrastructure at VinePair, direct brand marketing at Kickstarter, and lead organic growth at CookUnity. He approached these corporate roles as an undercover durational performance: a calculated effort to build his life out of poverty while auditing how complex systems scale from the inside out. That cycle recently closed with an intentional return to his body, breath, and voice. Drafting, performance training, live production, and corporate growth gave him direct experience of both physical capacity and institutional scale. The studio applies that evidence to the conditions around the artist: what can be sustained, where the system exceeds its limits, and what must change.

### The creative practice & literary output

https://wildlakestudio.com/about-founder/#founder-creative
