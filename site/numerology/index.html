Name & Number

Read the pattern.
Keep your own meaning.

What you carry. What you want. What the world encounters. Explore the numbers associated with your name and birth date, then bring your own experience to the reading.

A symbolic framework for reflection. Numbers are prompts, not verdicts.

01 // The material

Begin with a name.
Or a date. Or both.

A name gives Expression, Soul Urge, and Personality. A birth date gives Life Path and Birthday. Add both for Maturity.

Include all given names and surnames. Latin letters only; accents are folded into A–Z. Spaces and punctuation do not count.

How does Y sound in this name?

Leave unchecked to count Y as a consonant. Check it when Y carries a vowel sound. This choice applies to every Y in the name and changes Soul Urge and Personality, not Expression.

Life Path comes from your birth date, not your name.

Your inputs stay in this page. They are never saved or sent. Download a reading only if you choose to.

How reduction works

Add the selected letter values, then add the digits of the total until a single digit or a master number remains. We retain 11, 22, and 33 at each reduction and show their underlying single digit.

For Life Path, reduce month, day, and year separately, then add those results and reduce again. Birthday reduces the day alone. Maturity adds the final Expression and Life Path numbers, then reduces.

Numerology traditions differ, especially on Y, master numbers, and birth-date reduction. The steps here make this method explicit so you can compare it with another reading.

Accents are removed; common Latin forms such as Æ, Œ, and ß expand to AE, OE, and SS. For another writing system, enter your chosen Latin transliteration.
