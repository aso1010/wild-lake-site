Diagnostic intake

These prompts and fields are optional. Include the facts that help us identify the constraint, the engagement format, and the target horizon. You can also write directly by email.

If you are an artist

  • What is the core body of work, and what stage is it in?
  • Where is your current practice or infrastructure breaking down?
  • What conditions or timeline are you building toward over the next 12 months?

If you represent a label, gallery, agency or institution

  • Which artist, roster, or program requires support?
  • What is the operational bottleneck, release/exhibition timeline, or institutional demand?
  • Which engagement structure fits best (retainer, intensive, or fixed commission)?

Include if you can

A link to the work. Your name.
