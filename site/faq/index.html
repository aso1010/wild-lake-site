Questions institutions ask before they trust us with an artist.

How Wild-Lake works with artists, labels, galleries, and agencies. What you buy, who decides, what stays private, and what success looks like.

For Institutions

Every retained engagement begins with a fixed-scope architectural proposal. Depending on the artist's stage, deliverables include a written practice audit, a complete repertoire and catalog map, a six-month working rhythm and boundary protocol, or a bare-metal digital platform build — owned website, mailing list, archive, and release infrastructure.

You never buy vague stewardship. You buy concrete structural outputs with set review milestones.

In finished, durable output — not sentiment. Success looks like a coherent body of work, fewer abandoned projects, a release cadence the artist can sustain without burning out, and a live show built to survive a tour.

The artist is always the client of record, even when an institution funds the retainer.

All diagnostic sessions between Wild-Lake and the artist remain strictly confidential.

When a label, gallery, or agency funds the retainer, all three parties sign an upfront reporting agreement specifying exactly what gets shared back: structural milestones and delivery timelines, never raw private sessions.
