Client case · Emily Wilson

From content marketer to artist-writer on the food beat.

Emily Wilson writes about food, restaurants and the people who shape how we eat. She used to write gift guides for affiliate income. She now writes about who does the work behind the meal.

Emily’s client profile · Read The Angel

The practice

Practice

Food writing, photography

Work

The Angel, a newsletter about food, restaurants and the people who shape how we eat.

Terms

0% IP. 0% revenue share. She keeps the work.

01

The condition

Affiliate writing is paid by the purchase it produces. A gift guide has one job: move a reader toward a click. It does that job well. It also leaves no room for the people who grow, cook, serve and carry the meal.

The prose was good and the market was clear. The whole form pointed one way.

02

The movement

She changed the job. A marketer of objects became a writer with a subject: labor in food. Who is in the kitchen, who is in the field, who stands behind the counter.

Her eye and her voice stayed. The question changed, and so did the reader, and so did her relationship to her own work.

03

The reading

This is the studio's interpretation, not necessarily Emily's stated intention.

A writer paid per purchase answers to the sale. A writer who reports on labor answers to the subject. We read the first as a format and the second as a practice. A practice can be an artist's own.

04

The terms

Wild-Lake takes 0% IP ownership and 0% revenue share. The artist enters willingly and keeps creative control. Scope and fee are agreed before the work begins.
