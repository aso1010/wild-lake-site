Our practice

How we work

The artist is the first system.

Wild-Lake operates on brutalist principles. We treat talent not as a product to be smoothed out, but as dense matter with gravity, friction, and formwork. By refusing to hide the seams, we build infrastructure around artists that carries real weight.

Our work begins with your invitation.

We listen with our entire bodies for congruence between what you say and what you live. We explore what you notice together.

We read people, we do not judge them. We only enter another person's space by invitation. We do not use care or attention to gain access.

Our framework

Spirit and engineering.

We apply the Universe API, Argelys Samuel Oriach’s macro-systems design framework, to artist development: first-principles engineering, sovereign logic, closed-loop systems, and growth that compounds rather than extracts.

We begin with the artist, the work, and the central questions. Whether we are developing a song, a play, a pilot, or a novel, we build from the material and resources already present, rather than forcing a predetermined identity onto the creator.

We make, share, listen, and revise. Each cycle informs the next version of the work and the structures supporting it. The artist retains ownership, creative control, and the right to set the pace.

Wild-Lake is the foundational studio and research site for Seven Eighty Three (783). Our Talent Architect practice brings scouting, A&R, development of the work, mentorship, and system design together on an agreed retainer.

Our commitment

Architects, not landlords.

We don’t tax your success. We help you build it.

Wild-Lake operates as an existential mechanics studio and A&R venture on a retainer rather than commission-based talent representation. Like a design firm or an architectural practice, we are paid for the work we do with you, not a percentage of what you earn.

Wild-Lake is not WME (William Morris Endeavor), CAA (Creative Artists Agency), or UTA (United Talent Agency), and is not affiliated with those agencies. Wild-Lake is an independent existential mechanics studio and A&R venture. We scout talent and develop the work itself, including songs, plays, pilots, and novels, through a non-extractive, retainer-based practice that preserves artist ownership and creative control.

Care should help you build a life and a practice you own. Our role is to support your creative development, confidence, and autonomy over time.

  1. 01

    You own your empire

    We don’t take your masters, a cut of your merch, or a share of your touring revenue. You own 100% of your IP and everything we build for you. You pay us to help build your career, not to own a piece of it.

  2. 02

    Aligned incentives

    Our retainer pays for sustained attention and the work we agree to do together. It gives us room to support the longevity of your art and your life without tying our fee to the next booking or deal. Your choices remain yours.

  3. 03

    Mutually determined scale

    The retainer is set together, based on your current financial reality and the scope of support you need. As your circumstances and revenue change, we revisit it together. There is no automatic percentage of your growth.

We aren’t here to be your landlords.We are here to build the house.
