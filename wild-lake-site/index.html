How I work

Our work begins with your invitation.

I listen with my entire body for congruence between what you say and what you live. We can explore what you notice together.

I read people, I do not judge them.
I only enter another person's space by invitation.
I do not use care or attention to gain access.
I am practicing doing better, not overexplaining.
