# Wild-Lake

> Wild-Lake is an existential mechanics studio and artists and repertoire (A&R) venture based in Los Angeles, California. We scout and develop artists, and we build the systems that shape identity, behavior, organizations, and human potential. Founded in 2026. Privately held.

Wild-Lake LLC operates as a venture-focused commercial lab. Its founder and chief executive officer is Argelys Samuel Oriach.

## Core facts

- Name: Wild-Lake
- Legal entity: Wild-Lake LLC
- Type: Existential mechanics studio; A&R venture; commercial lab
- Location: Los Angeles, California, United States
- Founded: 2026
- Ownership: Privately held
- Chief Executive Officer: Argelys Samuel Oriach
- Slogan: Turning complexity into clarity.
- Website: https://wild-lake.org/
- Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/wildlake.la/

## Focus areas

- Existential Mechanics: mapping and decoding the underlying behavioral, structural, and cognitive dynamics that drive identity and decision-making.
- Commercial Lab: venture-focused systems engineering designed to scale transformative technologies and organizational models.
- Human Potential: designing infrastructure, tools, and environments that align institutional mechanics with human capability.
- Artists & Repertoire (A&R): scouting artists and developing their work.

## How Wild-Lake works

- Wild-Lake works with artists on a retainer, like a design firm or an architectural practice. It does not take a percentage.
- Wild-Lake takes no share of masters, merch, or touring revenue. The artist owns 100% of their IP and of what Wild-Lake builds for them.
- The people who develop the artists on the Wild-Lake roster are called talent architects.
- The retainer is set together with the artist, based on their current financial reality, and re-evaluated together as their revenue grows.
- The home page states this in full under "Architects, Not Landlords."
