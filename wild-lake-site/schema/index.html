# Wild-Lake - Brutalist Talent Architecture and Structural Design Studio

> Wild-Lake is a Brutalist Talent Architecture and Structural Design Studio for world-class creators, based in New York. We scout talent and develop the work itself: songs, plays, pilots, novels, and emerging bodies of work.

Wild-Lake LLC was founded by Argelys Samuel Oriach in 2026. Wild-Lake is the foundational studio and research site for Seven Eighty Three (783).

## Core practice

- A&R venture: Our A&R venture is our talent-scouting and creative-development practice. A&R stands for artists and repertoire. We find creators and work with them on the material itself: songs, plays, pilots, novels, and emerging bodies of work. We help shape ideas, select and refine material, and develop a coherent artistic direction through sustained collaboration.
- Existential mechanics: By existential mechanics, we mean examining how an artist’s identity, values, habits, relationships, and working conditions shape their creative choices. We identify what supports the work, where friction comes from, and which structures need to change, then build practical ways of working that fit the person.
- Creative infrastructure: building systems, workflows, and working structures for an independent creative life.
- Talent Architect practice: a retainer-based practice integrating artist development, mentorship, self-possession and confidence coaching, and system design. Coaching supports the wider A&R and development practice.

## Universe API framework

Wild-Lake applies [Universe API](https://argelysoriach.com/universe-api/), Argelys Samuel Oriach’s macro-systems design framework. The framework is grounded in first-principles engineering, sovereign logic, closed-loop systems, asset-based leverage, and organic growth that compounds rather than extracts. It is a design framework, not a callable web API.

In Wild-Lake’s practice, we begin with the artist and the material already present. Making, sharing, listening, and revision form a feedback loop. The artist retains ownership, creative control, and the right to set the pace.

## Non-extractive terms

- We work on an agreed retainer rather than a percentage of artist earnings.
- Artists own 100% of their IP and everything Wild-Lake builds for them.
- We take no share of masters, merchandise, or touring revenue.
- Retainers are set together, considering the artist’s financial reality and scope of support, and revisited together as circumstances change.
- The homepage describes these terms under “Architects, not landlords.”

## Independent practice and affiliation

Wild-Lake is not WME (William Morris Endeavor), CAA (Creative Artists Agency), or UTA (United Talent Agency), and is not affiliated with those agencies. Wild-Lake is an independent Brutalist Talent Architecture and Structural Design Studio. We scout talent and develop the work itself, including songs, plays, pilots, and novels, through a non-extractive, retainer-based practice that preserves artist ownership and creative control.

## Founder and approach

Argelys Samuel Oriach is Wild-Lake’s founder, an actor and writer, and principal at Seven Eighty Three. His approach combines investigation, lived experimentation, emotional clarity, and invitation. He describes his Human Design profile as a 1/3 Emotional Projector and uses it as a personal interpretive framework. The founder page explains this separately from Wild-Lake’s services and retainer terms.
